4aki uploader

4aki uploader is a private desktop tool used by its owner, Alexey Gruba, to manage his own two YouTube channels: 4aki_tv and Світ Цифрика.

It runs only on the owner's computer and uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • upload the owner's own videos with titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and scheduled publish dates;
  • add uploaded videos to the owner's playlists;
  • read new comments on the owner's channels and post replies.

The tool is not offered to the public and has no other users.
