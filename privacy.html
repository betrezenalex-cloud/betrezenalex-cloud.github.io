4aki uploader — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

4aki uploader ("the app") is a personal desktop tool operated by Alexey Gruba (contact: betrezenalex@gmail.com) for managing his own YouTube channels. It uses YouTube API Services.

Data the app accesses

  • Basic channel information (channel ID and title) of the signed-in account, to confirm the correct channel.
  • Video files, titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails that the owner chooses to upload.
  • Playlists of the owner's channel, to add uploaded videos to them.
  • Public comments on the owner's videos (author display name, text, date), to read and reply to them.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to perform the actions above on the owner's own channels. OAuth tokens and logs are stored locally on the owner's computer only. The app has no server, does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties, and does not use data for advertising.

Google and YouTube

By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings. Deleting the local token files removes all stored credentials. Locally stored data is deleted on request or when no longer needed, and in any case within 30 days of revocation.
